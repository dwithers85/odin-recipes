Nana's Saucy Weiners

A simple and delicious dish of hot dogs with a sweet and tangy sauce. Perfect over noodles or rice.

Modified from Nana K's recipes. Sauce ingredients have been doubled

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. wieners, sliced into 1/2" pieces
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp. mustard
  • 2 tbsp molasses
  • 1/2 c. barbecue sauce (substitute ketchup for less spice)
  • 1/2 c. sweet chili sauce
  • 1/2 c. water
  • Cooked rice or noodles (rotini or egg noodles are a family favorite)

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan, saute weiners and onion over medium-high heat until weiners browned and onion translucent
  2. Combine sauce ingredients: mustard, molasses, barbecue sauce (or ketchup), sweet chili sauce, and water.
  3. Pour sauce into saucepan with wieners and onions
  4. Cover and simmer for 10 min, stirry occasionally
  5. Serve on hot noodles or rice
