Hamburger Soup

Return to Homepage

Description

Hearty soup with hamburger, vegetables, and barley

Modified from Nana K's Recipe Book

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 3 cups beef broth
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 can tomato soup
  • 4 carrots, chopped finely
  • 3 celery stalks, chopped finely
  • 1 tsp parsley
  • 1/2 tsp thyme
  • salt
  • pepper
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2/3 cups barley

Instructions

  1. Brown Meat and onions
  2. Combine all ingredients in a large pot
  3. Simmer for 2 hours or all day
