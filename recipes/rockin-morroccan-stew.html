Rockin' Moroccan Stew

Return to Odin Recipes

Description

Moroccan vegetable stew with sweet potatoes, chickpeas and ginger

Modified from Reader's Digest Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 1 cup onions, chopped
  • 1/2 cup celery, diced
  • 1/2 cup green pepper, chopped
  • 1 tsp garlic, minced
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 3 cups sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 can (19 oz/540 mL) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp gingerroot, grated
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup raisins (optional)
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large nonstick saucepan over medium-high heat
  2. Add onions, celery, green pepper and garlic
  3. Cook and stir until vegetables being to soften, about 3 minutes
  4. Add all remaining ingredients, except raisins, peanut butter and cilantro
  5. Bring to a boil and simmer on low, covered for 20 minutes
  6. Stir in raisins, peanut butter, and cilantro
  7. Mix well. Simmer for 5 more minutes
  8. Serve hot.
